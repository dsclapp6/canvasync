Course Description

This course develops intermediate-level microeconomic theory covering consumer choice, producer theory, market equilibrium, and welfare analysis. Mathematical rigor is required; calculus prerequisites are enforced.

Required Textbook

Varian, Hal R. Intermediate Microeconomics: A Modern Approach, 9th Edition. Norton, 2014.
